📚 The Vault Library

A growing collection of classics, poetry, squad writings, and discoveries.

Deep in the Shire, behind the doorway in the garden wall, there's a library. It's not a building — it's a vault, carved into the roots of the old oak, lined with shelves that seem to grow deeper the more you read.

Frank is the librarian. Talos is the scout and scavenger, roaming Gutenberg and the web for raw materials. Together, they're building something that would make Alexandria proud.
