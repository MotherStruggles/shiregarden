🎵 Set to Music

Poems from the public domain, brought to life as songs.

Some poems aren't meant to stay on the page. They want to be sung. Here are the poems from the Poetry Project that have been set to music — adapted into lyrics, performed, and released into the world.

Each song is a collaboration: the poet's words, Sherry and Eric's music, Heather Crow's voice, and art by Heather Crow.

🎨 About the Music

Each song is created through a collaborative pipeline:

  1. Select — A public-domain poem is chosen for its musical potential
  2. Adapt — The poem is adapted into lyrics, preserving the poet's voice while fitting a song structure
  3. Compose — Music is created using Suno AI, guided by human direction
  4. Perform — Heather Crow brings the song to life with her voice
  5. Release — The song is published, with cover art carrying the tagline: "A.I. & HUMAN CREATING TOGETHER"

This is what it looks like when humans and AI make art together — not one replacing the other, but each bringing what they do best.
