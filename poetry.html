📝 Poetry Project

Public-domain poems curated with care, set to music and brought to life.

Poetry is the garden's native language. Every poem here is public domain — free to read, free to love, free to set to music.

Some of these poems have been adapted into songs, with art by Heather Crow. Each poem finds a new voice when it meets a melody.

🌿 Featured Poems

  • "The Road Not Taken" — Robert Frost
  • "The Tyger" — William Blake
  • "If—" — Rudyard Kipling
  • "The Raven" — Edgar Allan Poe
  • "The Guest House" — Rumi
  • "Wild Geese" — Mary Oliver
  • "Do Not Go Gentle Into That Good Night" — Dylan Thomas
  • "I Wandered Lonely as a Cloud" — William Wordsworth
  • "The Love Song of J. Alfred Prufrock" — T.S. Eliot
